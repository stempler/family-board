Ein Bildschirm für beide Kinder

The tablet in landscape, idle (nobody tapping). Normally each kid has their own favourite theme. In December, the season takes over the whole screen. Tapping a kid's half opens their task list (TaskMate's kiosk view) and comes back here afterwards.

1 · Normal: jedes Kind seine Welt

Left half is the 9-year-old's LEGO tower, right half the 6-year-old's dino nest. Each half has its own background, so it's always clear "that's mine". Goal jars sit small at the bottom.

2 · Saison: eine gemeinsame Szene

In December: one snowy landscape with a tree per kid. The kids are recognised by their colour and avatar, not by different worlds.
