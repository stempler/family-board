Wie sollen die Punkte aussehen?

Three ways of showing points, all with the same Christmas theme and two kids (12 and 7 points). Only the idea matters here, not the polish. Click the ones you like, then tell me in the terminal what works and what doesn't.

A · Sammeln

Each point is one more ornament on the kid's own tree. You see "a lot" at a glance, and counting isn't needed. Spending removes ornaments.

B · Weg zur Belohnung

A winding path like a board game. Each kid's figure moves one stone per point toward a reward (e.g. 📺 screen time). This shows what's coming next.

C · Glas füllen

A cookie jar fills up. A line on the glass marks "enough for a reward". Like A, but with a clear goal.
